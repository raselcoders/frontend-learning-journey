How to be a Web Developer

To become a web developer, start by learning the basics of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. These languages build the structure, style, and interactivity of websites. Practice by creating simple projects like personal pages or small apps. Learn responsive design and basic version control with Git. Explore front-end frameworks or back-end technologies as you grow. Study how the web works, including browsers and servers. Build a portfolio to show your skills. Keep learning through tutorials and documentation. Stay curious, practice regularly, and solve real problems to improve and succeed.
